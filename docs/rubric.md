# Final Defense Rubric (source: CDK ITE Capstone Project and Research Final Defense Form)

Scale: 5 = Excellent, 4 = Very Good, 3 = Good (Meets Expectations), 2 = Fair (Needs Improvement), 1 = Poor.
Weighted score of a criterion = rating × weight. Weights total 100%.

## A. The System (Project Output) — 45%

### 1. Functionality & Correctness — 20% (group)
Guide: Does it work? Is the output accurate? Does it meet the objectives from Ch. 1?
- 5: Error-free. All features promised in the Scope (Ch 1) are 100% functional, and all outputs are correct.
- 4: Most features work. Only minor, non-critical bugs were found.
- 3: The core features work. However, some minor features are buggy or incomplete.
- 2: Even the core features have major bugs. The output is unreliable.
- 1: The system is non functional or crashes during the demo.

### 2. Technical Complexity — 15% (group)
Guide: Is this Capstone-level? Or just a simple CRUD? Is the code/architecture well-implemented?
- 5: Clearly advanced (Capstone-level). Excellent implementation of the technology stack. Far exceeds a simple CRUD.
- 4: Demonstrates clear complexity. Not just CRUD. Good use of the chosen tools.
- 3: "Acceptable." Meets the minimum requirements for a Capstone project.
- 2: Too simple. Appears to be mostly CRUD. Does not look like a Capstone.
- 1: This is a minor subject's project.

### 3. Usability (UI/UX) — 10% (group)
Guide: Is it easy to use? Does it look professional and clean?
- 5: Highly professional, clean, and intuitive. Easy to use even without a manual.
- 4: Looks good. Easy to use. Only 1-2 minor points of confusion.
- 3: "Acceptable." It is functional, but the design is very basic or dated.
- 2: Difficult to use. The workflow is confusing. The UI is cluttered.
- 1: Unprofessional design. Very hard to navigate.

## B. The Manuscript (Final Document – Chapters 1-5) — 20%

### 4. Manuscript Quality — 20% (group)
Guide: Is it complete (Ch 1-5)? Is Ch 4 (Results) clear? Is Ch 5 (Conclusion) valid? Is the writing professional?
- 5: Perfect. Complete, well-written. Ch 4 & 5 are perfectly aligned with Ch 1. Excellent grammar and formatting.
- 4: Complete. Only minor revisions (grammar, format) needed. Ch 4 & 5 are clear.
- 3: Complete. However, Ch 4 & 5 are somewhat "weak" or lack deep analysis. Some errors.
- 2: Incomplete. Important sections (e.g., Appendices, Ch 5) are missing.
- 1: Poorly written, incomplete, and disorganized.

## C. The Oral Defense (Presentation) — 35%
(The paper form mislabels this section as "B".)

### 5. Live Demonstration — 15% (per proponent)
Guide: How was the system demo? Was it smooth? Was the explanation clear?
- 5: Flawless demo. Clear, confident, and professional explanation of all features. Highlighted the "best features" well.
- 4: Smooth demo, but the explanation was a bit nervous or disorganized.
- 3: "Acceptable." Showed the features, but didn't really "sell" the value or purpose.
- 2: Encountered technical problems (crashed, slow) during the demo. Panicked.
- 1: Could not properly demonstrate the system.

### 6. Mastery & Q&A Handling — 20% (per proponent)
Guide: Did they answer the panel? Do they know the technical details (code, database)?
- 5: Experts. Answered all questions (even highly technical ones) clearly, directly, and professionally.
- 4: Answered most questions well. Struggled with 1-2 very technical questions.
- 3: Answered general questions. For technical (code) questions, only the "leader" knew the answer.
- 2: Could not answer technical questions. Seemed confused.
- 1: Did not know the answers. Relied only on the system to work.

## Panel's Decision
| Decision | Range | Note |
|---|---|---|
| PASSED | 4.5 – 5.0 | Approved. Submit final hardbound. |
| PASSED with MINOR REVISIONS | 3.5 – 4.4 | Must revise the system/manuscript. To be checked by Adviser/Panel. |
| PASSED with MAJOR REVISIONS | 2.5 – 3.4 | Not yet passed. Must fix major bugs and re-defend. |
| FAILED | Below 2.5 | Must re-enroll in Capstone 2. |
